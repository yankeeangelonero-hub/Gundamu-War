# US-001 — Author a body and a mind, then watch them fight

As an Engineer (Player), I can author a mech's **body** (kitbash parts under a weight/power
budget) and its **mind** (a body-gated behaviour deck), then watch a deterministic duel whose
outcome I can read from the fight and a debrief, so that I can forge a signature ace and learn
how to make it better.

Its falsifiable claims — the same `{body, deck, opponent, seed}` reproduces a byte-identical
fight; a card whose required part is absent is marked dead and never fires; and the outcome is
never presented without a concrete in-fight reason (dead card, whiff out of range, or
hesitation) — are recorded as the checkable spine of Journey D1 and become this story's slice
acceptance checks.
